About Us

Japanese Manufacturing at System 5/95

My name is Akio Nakashima. I am responsible for managing the manufacturing processes of System 5/95 in Japan.

For us, manufacturing is not simply the assembly of a device. It is a complete and carefully structured system in which every detail follows its own path: from engineering concept and development to production, quality inspection, service support, and preparation for delivery to the customer.

At System 5/95, our manufacturing process is built around five key areas: design and development, manufacturing, quality assurance, service, and logistics.

Each area plays an important role, because a true Japanese product cannot be created by chance. It requires discipline, precision, responsibility, and constant attention to detail.

Design and Development

Every product begins with an engineering idea.

At the design stage, we think not only about the functions of the device, but also about how a person will use it every day: whether it is comfortable, safe, intuitive, reliable, and pleasant to use.

The Japanese approach to development means that form, materials, movement, temperature, ergonomics, and the overall feeling of the product must work together as one complete system.

For us, innovation is not simply about adding new technologies. True innovation must be useful, stable, and natural for the person using it.

Manufacturing

Turning the engineering idea into a finished product with precision.

The manufacturing department is responsible for turning the engineering idea into a finished product with precision.

In Japanese manufacturing, there are no small details. Even a slight difference in a component, assembly, or user experience can affect the overall quality of the device.

That is why we focus on assembly accuracy, component stability, careful production processes, and consistent results.

Our goal is for every System 5/95 device to meet one clear standard of quality: reliable, thoughtfully made, and worthy of trust.

Quality Assurance

Quality begins at the very first stage.

For us, quality is not just a final inspection at the end of production. Quality begins at the very first stage.

From development to assembly, we evaluate reliability, safety, operational stability, and ease of use.

Every device must not only function properly, but also be ready for long-term daily use.

That is why quality assurance is a separate and essential part of our manufacturing structure.

Service

Responsibility does not end at the moment of purchase.

For us, responsibility does not end at the moment of purchase.

We create devices for long-term use, which is why service support is an important part of the System 5/95 philosophy.

Our approach to service is based on the Japanese principle of respect for the customer: a product should not only work well on the first day, but also maintain its reliability, stability, and value for many years.

That is why we offer a 5-year warranty. For us, this is not simply a commercial condition, but a reflection of our confidence in the quality of Japanese manufacturing, the reliability of our engineering solutions, and the durability of our devices.

We want every customer to feel calm and confident when choosing System 5/95. When someone invests in a premium device for daily body care, they should know that behind the product stands a team that takes responsibility for its quality.

Our Manufacturing Philosophy

Made in Japan is not simply a statement. It is a responsibility.

System 5/95 is built on the principles of Japanese manufacturing: precision, stability, reliability, service, and respect for the user.

We believe that a premium device must be thoughtfully created not only technically, but also emotionally. It should inspire trust, feel pleasant to use, and become a natural part of daily self-care.

For us, Made in Japan is not simply a statement of where a product is made. It is a responsibility to every person who chooses our product.

It means attention to detail, quality control, engineering discipline, long-term service support, and the commitment to creating devices that serve beautifully, reliably, and for many years.

The 5-year System 5/95 warranty is an expression of our confidence that Japanese quality should be more than a promise — it should be real support for the customer over many years.
